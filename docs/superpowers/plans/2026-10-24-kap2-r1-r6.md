# KAP-2 R1/R6 implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task by task, then obtain an independent code review.

**Goal:** Fix the two bounded local recovery defects without running a remote experiment or activating Knowledge.

**Architecture:** Witness recovery installs default-deny while volatile admission remains CLOSED. Home resets and reads its protected head under its partition lane, submits that exact proof to `confirm_home(partition, incarnation, home_head)`, and calls `open_admission(partition, incarnation)` only after confirmation enters REAUTH_ONLY. Both transitions recheck pinned connections, the process lock, partition/incarnation and the witness default-deny row. A procedure-owned incarnation registry preserves every observed recovery head, including incarnations with no events; retained events also reject collisions.

**Tech stack:** Python 3.11+, PyMySQL, MariaDB stored procedures, pytest, local WSL.

**Spec:** `docs/architecture/reviews/2026-10-24-knowledge-kap2-recovery-review.md` R1/R6; runtime contract §19; incarnation amendment recovery sequence.

## Review focus

- Home connection, reset, readback or lane failure must leave both admissions closed, including recovery from a previously open state.
- A competing reader or serving mutation cannot proceed during Home reset or REAUTH_ONLY.
- A stale or wrong-partition confirmation/open cannot admit a different recovery generation.
- Lost connections and changed witness head between confirmation and opening must deny.
- Incarnations with events, without events, initial heads and retained event-only history must reject reuse transactionally.
