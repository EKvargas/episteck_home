# Olin Finance: lowest-maintenance architecture review

**Date:** 2026-10-05. **Status:** research input, not an accepted architecture decision. **Olin checkout inspected:** `140147e84a8b8f7b317702a0d2058048f91afffd`. Upstream source revisions are pinned below. No software, deployment, provider data, or credentials were changed. The documented Nuremberg SSH endpoint timed out during a read-only check, so current production state is **not** independently verified here.

## 1. Process and current boundary

Use the proposed sequence: human evidence or statement → proposed fact → human confirmation → reconciled financial fact → permission-filtered read model → Olin tool/UI. A WhatsApp paste, screenshot, or future bot message is evidence, not an instruction to post money. A reported repayment is not a verified receipt. Reconciliation compares confirmed records against the source statement and keeps corrections traceable.

The [canonical architecture](../ARCHITECTURE.md), [data ownership rule](../DATA_OWNERSHIP.md), [consent policy](../SECURITY_AND_CONSENT.md), and [ADRs 0001, 0002, 0009](../adr/) put Person/Circle/Consent in the Ashburn Frappe Home Control Plane and domain truth in independent services. The company ERP is a separate site, not a household ledger. The [deployment record](../DEPLOYMENT.md) describes Nuremberg rootless Podman/Quadlet domain services, the EU Home BFF, Home MCP, unprivileged Hermes home-agent, Tailscale cross-node authorization, and no cross-region database joins. The BFF holds human OAuth tokens and issues single-use delegated context; domain APIs independently call Home policy before sensitive reads/writes. Neither an agent tool nor a client may supply the acting Person ([actor API](../../../apps/episteck_home/episteck_home/api.py), [Nutrition enforcement pattern](../../../services/nutrition/app/service.py), [Home MCP](../../../services/home-mcp/home_mcp/server.py)).

The repository has **no Finance service, Finance API, Finance MCP tool, or Finance UI implementation** ([status](../STATUS.md), [roadmap](../ROADMAP.md)). The Next.js 16/React 19 Home Hub and Ask Olin view are mock-backed; its proposed same-origin BFF integration is a design, not deployed functionality ([UI integration design](../../product/HOME_HUB_INTEGRATION_FOUNDATION.md), [chat source](../../../apps/home-hub/src/app/ask/AskOlin.tsx)). The existing `FINANCE` consent domain is a policy vocabulary, not Finance data or access to Ana's account. The Home site uses Frappe/ERPNext v15 in the [deployment record](../DEPLOYMENT.md); existing backend patterns are Python 3.11+, FastAPI, FastMCP, SQLite, and a repository abstraction for Nutrition. Frappe owns identity and consent. Finance should have its own store and source-of-truth boundary. A prior [Core proposal](../../episteck-home/core-proposal.md) says Finance should begin read only and avoid ERP postings; the requested repayment case management goes beyond that wording and needs architect disposition before implementation.

The existing [BFF delegation audiences](../../../services/home-bff/home_bff/sessions.py) are `home-control-plane` and `svc-nutrition`; the [gateway configuration](../../../deploy/gateway/nginx-mcp-gateway.conf) has Home and Nutrition routes, not Finance. The [internal token mint](../../../services/home-bff/home_bff/internal_app.py) and [Nutrition Home-policy client](../../../services/nutrition/app/home_control/client.py) show a reusable authorization pattern, but a Finance audience, route, service principal, permission checks and deployment still have to be designed and tested. `check_access_many` covers several requirements for one subject per delegated request; it does not settle access to a case involving several people. The timed-out server inspection leaves even the documented services' **live state unverified**.

## 2. Domain model and services/APIs

| Concept | Required distinction and invariant |
| --- | --- |
| Financial account and snapshot | Owner, currency, source, effective date and observed/imported date. Sparkasse cash; Trade Republic cash **separate from** positions; Ana's N26 belongs to Ana; US USD bank and physical USD cash are separate accounts. Unknown/stale is never zero. |
| Position and valuation | Units, security/plan, price/FX source and as-of dates; EquatePlus available and restricted shares separately. An estimated value is not liquid cash. |
| Money movement | Preserve source rows and stable import keys. Link both sides of an internal transfer and exclude it from spending. Classify expenses and subscriptions separately; a remittance to the friend is an advance or gift, not automatically household expense. |
| Exchange case | Requester, beneficiary, debtor, payer and friend are separate roles. Several cases may allocate to one EUR payout; retain VES delivery evidence and date. A delivered reimbursable case can remain `USD_AMOUNT_UNCONFIRMED` until the later agreement. Gifts create no receivable. |
| Debt and repayment | Agreed USD obligation, including opening debt with incomplete history and explicit opening-balance provenance. Partial USD bank/cash receipts may allocate across several obligations. Reported payment, verified receipt, and allocation are separate states/events. No allocation can exceed verified funds or debt outstanding. |
| Correction/evidence | Original artifact reference/hash, who confirmed, timestamps and reversal/supersession links. Do not silently overwrite confirmed amounts. Duplicate keys combine source ID or source-file hash/row with account/date/amount checks and human review of ambiguous matches. |

A small Finance API can expose separate commands for `propose`, `confirm`, `import-preview`, `reconcile`, `record-delivery`, `agree-debt`, `verify-receipt`, `allocate-repayment`, and `reverse/correct`, plus permission-filtered overview/case/debt reads. Keep currency arithmetic in integer minor units or `Decimal`, record actual FX consideration/fees separately, and never infer USD debt from an EUR→VES conversion. Confirmed money movements should be balanced within their currency or explicitly paired with FX/fee legs; immutable source evidence and append-only correction history make imported and manual entries auditable. Finance checks Home `FINANCE` action permissions independently; multi-person cases need a deliberate intersection/redaction policy before retrieval.

The home-agent should receive narrow business-safe Finance tools, using the same opaque delegation transport as Nutrition. Tool arguments may name a **subject**, case, or proposal but never the acting Person, bank credentials, a generic SQL query, or a payment initiation route. The UI should show source/as-of date per balance and valuation, separate Trade Republic cash/holdings and restricted shares, and show `unconfirmed`, `reported`, and `verified` states plainly. A Home Hub context switch is presentation state only, not authorization. WhatsApp paste and screenshot upload can start the proposal path; Telegram ingestion is optional.

## 5. Reuse, statement import and Sparkasse API

**Reuse in the manual core.** The repo already has [Frappe Person/Circle/Consent](../../../apps/episteck_home/episteck_home/api.py), [BFF delegation](../../../services/home-bff/home_bff/sessions.py), [Home MCP](../../../services/home-mcp/home_mcp/server.py), a [Nutrition FastAPI/service pattern](../../../services/nutrition/app/service.py), and a Next.js Home Hub shell. Python 3.11's [`Decimal`](https://docs.python.org/3.11/library/decimal.html), [`sqlite3`](https://docs.python.org/3.11/library/sqlite3.html), [`csv.DictReader`](https://docs.python.org/3.11/library/csv.html) and [`hashlib`](https://docs.python.org/3.11/library/hashlib.html) cover exact values, transactions, a narrowly mapped CSV and fingerprints. FastAPI/Pydantic/httpx/FastMCP are already in the stack. This is reuse of interfaces and libraries, not evidence of a deployed Finance system. [py-moneyed's source](https://github.com/py-moneyed/py-moneyed/blob/4c90ed511d0a768490f2351455a642700926753c/src/moneyed/classes.py) offers currency-safe arithmetic but adds a dependency for a small value-object saving; revisit if mixed-currency bugs justify it. Do not import Beancount merely for snapshot holdings.

**One statement importer, separately estimated.** Target a *Sparkasse booked-transaction CSV* after obtaining a redacted current export from this account. A [public 2018 Sparkasse Freiburg test fixture, revision `9e3213c`](https://github.com/omarkohl/ofxstatement-sparkasse-freiburg/blob/9e3213ce80bd73084b233cea2b925fd0b93d3a04/tests/data/simple1.csv), actually inspected, has quoted semicolon-separated `Buchungstag`, `Valutadatum`, `Betrag`, `Waehrung`, `Info` and counterparty/reference columns; amounts use decimal commas and dates `dd.mm.yy`. It includes both `Umsatz vorgemerkt` (pending) and `Umsatz gebucht` (booked), so posting every row would be wrong. Its [converter code](https://github.com/omarkohl/ofxstatement-sparkasse-freiburg/blob/9e3213ce80bd73084b233cea2b925fd0b93d3a04/src/ofxstatement/plugins/germany_sparkasse_freiburg.py) uses `csv.DictReader`, `Decimal` and a hash but hardcodes local details and does not filter pending rows; adapting the plugin is less attractive than a small format adapter. A different [regional Sparkasse lists CSV exports](https://www.sparkasse-westmuensterland.de/fi/home/login-online-banking/uebersicht-glossare.html?lightbox=%2Ffi%2Fhome%2Fonlinebanking%2Fumsaetze%2Fumsaetze%2Fglossar-umsaetze%3Fn%3Dtrue), but **neither source proves the user's 2026 format**. The acceptance gate is a real redacted sample: exact header/encoding, booked/pending semantics, sign, date, currency and stable reference. Then preview, deduplicate, reconcile with manual records and confirm before posting. Indicative **3–6 engineer-days after a compatible sample**, outside the 30–50 days; format mismatch or PDF-only export requires re-estimation. No sample is available yet.

**Sparkasse API is the first bank connection increment.** Sparkasse's [XS2A operator](https://www.starfinanz.de/services/xs2a/) confirms a Berlin Group REST interface for consented payment accounts and requires a licensed/registered third-party provider and eIDAS certificate for production access. That is not an unrestricted personal API credential. An account-information aggregator is the likely lower-maintenance route if its contract allows this household use and supports the exact institution. [GoCardless Bank Account Data](https://docs.gocardless.com/docs/bank-account-data/quickstart-guide) documents institution discovery, user consent and account/balance/transaction APIs; its default agreement is 90 days of history/access. [finAPI](https://documentation.finapi.io/xs2a/05-how-to-access-account-balances-and-transactions) documents account, balance and transaction access subject to valid consent and bank-side coverage. Its [published prices](https://www.finapi.io/preise/) as inspected on 2026-10-05 include €60/month B2C access plus a PSD2 licence for non-own accounts, or €200/month for up to ten own accounts; whether a private household with Ana's separately owned account qualifies for either arrangement is **unverified**, so neither is an approved quote. Direct FinTS via [python-fints reading API](https://github.com/raphaelm/python-fints/blob/master/docs/reading.rst) is a fallback to investigate only if the particular Sparkasse supports it and credential/SCA operation is acceptable; it makes Olin responsible for bank protocol and secret maintenance. Provider selection requires the exact Sparkasse, account type, consent owner, read-only scope, support terms, price and one successful non-production consent test. Keep provider tokens out of agent tools, segregate them by owner, and show sync status/failure without replacing confirmed records. Indicative implementation **5–10 engineer-days after provider access and contract**, plus an unknown provider fee and roughly **0.5–1.5 engineering days/month** for consent renewal, API changes and failed-sync triage. Procurement/consent can dominate calendar duration. This increment imports balances and booked transactions only; it never initiates payments.

## 7. Recommendation and architect review

Choose **approach C provisionally**: a narrow Olin-owned Finance domain, with standard exact-money/CSV facilities and the existing identity/delegation service pattern. Increment 1 is the first independently useful deliverable: it gives dated, attributable financial visibility without an accounting engine or bank agreement. Its **12–20 engineer-days** assume a working Home identity/policy path and a minimal authenticated screen; recurring engineering is roughly **0.25–0.5 day/month** for backup checks, dependencies and permission review, plus human data-entry time. The full manual release is the conditional **30–50 engineer-days** above; recurring engineering after all four increments is roughly **0.5–1.5 days/month**. These are hypotheses for planning, not an accepted architecture decision or a verified production delivery plan.

Architect review must settle: (1) whether the write-capable case ledger supersedes the older “read only, no postings” Finance pilot wording; (2) ownership, disclosure and revocation for multi-person cases and Ana's N26, including household totals; (3) whether one EUR payer's advance may be apportioned among several debtors and how gifts are evidenced; (4) whether VES delivery/rate is evidence only or requires formal friend reconciliation; (5) the exact Sparkasse institution/account, aggregator eligibility and acceptable monthly cost, including Ana's separately owned account later; (6) US bank identity, current authorized statement samples and EquatePlus restriction fields; (7) required valuation and tax/cost-basis depth; and (8) whether Home Hub auth and the Nuremberg deployment match the repository. **Server status remains unverified** because read-only SSH inspection did not succeed. No implementation, deployment, data change or bank connection was attempted.
